Our Approach

Learning that Goes Deeper

At St. Lawrence, we follow the Uganda National Curriculum framework while enriching it with creative, practical, and values-based learning experiences. We believe every child learns differently, and our teachers are trained to meet learners where they are.

From Baby Class through Primary Seven, we ensure a smooth, well-supported academic journey with regular assessments, feedback to parents, and extra support for learners who need it.

School Sections

Three Stages of Excellent Learning

Each section is tailored to the developmental needs and learning style of the child at that stage.

Nursery Section
Baby Class · Middle Class · Top Class (Ages 3–5)

Our nursery section provides a warm, play-based environment where children develop language, social, motor, and early literacy and numeracy skills through structured play, songs, stories, and creative activities.

Learning Areas

Language Activities Number Work Creative Arts Physical Play Environmental Studies Music & Rhymes Social Skills
Lower Primary
P1 · P2 · P3 (Ages 6–9)

In Lower Primary, learners build strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking. Our teachers use activity-based learning methods that make core subjects engaging and deeply understood.

Subjects Offered

English Mathematics Science Social Studies Religious Education Luganda Physical Education Creative Arts
Upper Primary
P4 · P5 · P6 · P7 (Ages 10–14)

Upper Primary prepares learners for the Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) with intensive subject coaching, mock exams, and mentorship. Our P7 results consistently outperform district averages.

PLE Subjects

English Language Mathematics Science Social Studies & MRE Agriculture Computer Studies Physical Education
Beyond the Classroom

Co-Curricular Activities

We develop well-rounded learners through a rich variety of extracurricular programmes.

Football & Sports

Inter-class and inter-school competitions in football, netball, athletics, and more.

Music & Choir

Drama & Debate

Boosting confidence, communication, and critical thinking through performance.

Hands-on computer literacy sessions preparing learners for the digital world.

Reading Club

Weekly reading sessions and book reviews to build a culture of reading and imagination.
