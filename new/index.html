Why St. Lawrence

The Difference You Can Feel

Experienced, Passionate Teachers

Our team of over 40 qualified educators brings warmth, knowledge, and dedication to every classroom. We believe the teacher-student relationship is the foundation of great learning.

  • All teachers are government-certified
  • Regular professional development trainings
  • Low teacher-to-student ratio

Safe, Stimulating Environment

We maintain a clean, safe, and organised school environment where children thrive. From secure playgrounds to well-stocked classrooms, every space is designed with your child in mind.

  • Secure school compound
  • Safe playgrounds and sports areas
  • Regular health and hygiene checks

Holistic Education Beyond the Classroom

We go beyond books. From football and athletics to music, drama, and community service, we nurture the whole child — building confidence, teamwork, and character alongside academics.

  • Sports, music, drama & arts
  • Annual cultural & sports events
  • Environmental & community programs
