Our Story

Built on Community, Driven by Purpose

St. Lawrence Nursery and Primary School was founded with a single, powerful conviction: every child in Kyamunara deserves access to high-quality education delivered by teachers who truly care.

Over the years, we have grown from a small neighbourhood school into one of the most trusted educational institutions in the Masindi District — a school where parents confidently bring their children knowing they will be nurtured, challenged, and loved.

Today, we proudly serve over 500 learners from Nursery through Primary Seven, guided by a team of qualified, dedicated educators who go beyond the syllabus to inspire a genuine love of learning.

What Guides Us

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To provide quality, child-centred education that develops the whole child — academically, socially, morally, and physically — empowering every learner to realise their full potential.

Our Vision

To be the leading nursery and primary school in Masindi District, recognised for excellence in education, character formation, and community impact.

Core Values

  • Excellence in all we do
  • Integrity & Honesty
  • Compassion & Belonging
  • Responsibility & Service
  • Faith & Moral Grounding
