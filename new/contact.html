Common Questions

What are the school fees?

Fee structures vary by class level and term. Please contact us directly or visit the school for the current fee schedule. We also offer payment plans and bursaries for qualifying families.

Do you offer boarding facilities?

Currently St. Lawrence operates as a day school. We open early at 7:00am and have extended care available until 5:30pm for working parents.

When does the school year begin?

The Ugandan school year runs in three terms: Term 1 starts in February, Term 2 in June, and Term 3 in September. We accept new learners at the beginning of each term.
