Who Can Apply

Admission Requirements

We accept learners from Baby Class through Primary Seven. Here is what you need to know before applying.

Age Requirements

  • • Baby Class: 3 years and above
  • • Middle Class: 4 years and above
  • • Top Class: 5 years and above
  • • Primary 1: 6 years and above

Documents Required

  • • Birth certificate (copy)
  • • 2 passport photos of child
  • • Previous school report (if any)
  • • Parent/guardian ID copy

Intake Dates

  • • Term 1: February each year
  • • Term 2: June each year
  • • Term 3: September each year
  • • Mid-term transfers considered
